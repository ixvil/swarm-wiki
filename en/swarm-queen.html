Swarm Queen

Swarm Queen is the final boss of Moonlit Meadow. It arrives at 10:00 (a “WARNING” banner shows 3 s before) and killing it clears the world and ends the run as a victory.

Speed 70 units/s, body radius 64, knockback resistance 97%. Boss HP does not grow with time and ignores the kill-speed surge. Contact damage is fixed when the boss spawns; shots deal half of it and your armor counts half against them.

Attacks

The boss chases you and picks an attack whenever its timer runs out (first after 4 s). Every charge is telegraphed for 0.75 s (a line on the ground), then the boss dashes at 4.8× its speed. After a charge it rests 3.6 s.

  • “THE QUEEN CALLS!”: a ring of 14 bats around you with a gap toward the emptiest side (40% of decisions).
  • Ring of 20 shots in every direction when closer than 520 units (next 35%).
  • Otherwise a telegraphed charge.

How to beat it

Leave every bat ring through its gap before it closes; step through the space between ring shots. The Queen is the final boss of the Meadow — killing her clears the world and unlocks Lightning, Vega the Stormcaller, the Curse and the Bone Desert.

  • Lightning and Storm Lord send 30% of strikes to a boss on the field.
  • A bomb pickup deals 8% of a boss's max HP.
  • Guardian Nova only stuns bosses for 0.4 s; it does not push them.
  • While a final boss is alive, regular spawns are halved and the minimum on-screen swarm is switched off: the finale is a duel.
  • Running away does not work: beyond 350 units a final boss speeds up, up to ×2.2.
  • A final boss drops nothing — the run ends as a win with a clear bonus of 3,000 points.
