Vitality

From the Swarm Survivor: Horde Hunter Wiki · data from game version 1.7.2
VitalityArmory upgrade
Armory
Per level+12% max HP per level
Levels5
Full price3,650 coins
At level 5+60% max HP

Vitality is one of the six permanent upgrade lines in the Armory: “+12% max HP per level”.

Multiplies max HP by 1 + 0.12 per level (×1.6 at level 5: a 70 HP hero gets 112 HP). It also applies in challenge rounds. More max HP also means bigger heals from hearts and level-ups, which heal a share of max HP.
