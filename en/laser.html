Laser is one of the eight weapons in Swarm Survivor. In-game card: “A beam burns through a line”. It levels up to 5 and evolves into Prism Ray with Critical Eye.

How it works

A beam fires at the nearest enemy within 420 units (or ahead) and stays for 0.5 s, damaging everything along the line every 0.1 s — 5 ticks per shot. At level 5 a second beam fires in the opposite direction. Length is capped at 312 units.

Twin Souls adds one more beam to this weapon, including the evolved form.

Evolution

Reach level 5 and own Critical Eye at any level: the next level-up offers the Prism Ray card, or the next chest evolves it automatically. Three beams 120° apart that last 0.9 s (9 ticks) and sweep around the hero at 1 rad/s.

Tactics

  • The beam lines up with the nearest enemy and burns everything behind it, so it shines when enemies queue up behind each other: kite so that the swarm forms a line.
  • Ticks are small, so late armor hurts the laser; Critical Eye (its evolution partner) doubles damage on crits.

How to get it

Find the prism shard in the Ruins (12:00); or buy for 12,000 coins once Cursed Ruins is reached.

Unlocking Laser also adds Critical Eye to the passive pool.

See Collection and weapon unlocks for how locked weapons work.
