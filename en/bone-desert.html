Bone Desert

Bone Desert is world 2 of 4. Ghosts replace part of the slimes, spitters arrive at 2:00, bombers at 3:00, chargers at 4:00 and rocks from 3:00. Every elite regenerates 2.5% of its max HP per second, and bomber blasts leave burning pools.

The first rock of each strike lands where you stand; extra rocks land 70–200 units around you. Each shows its shadow 1.1 s before impact. With the Curse on, strikes come 15% more often. See Hazards.

Strategy

Regenerating elites need burst damage — chip damage just heals back. Stay out of the fire pools and keep in mind that the Mines pedestal appears at 9:00 if you have not unlocked Mines yet.

Unlocks

Requires clearing the Meadow. Clearing it opens Cursed Ruins and the hero Nyx the Rogue. Reaching the Desert also lets you buy Mines and Shockwave in the Collection and weapon unlocks.
