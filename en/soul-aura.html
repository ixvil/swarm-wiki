Soul Aura

Soul Aura is the evolved form of Garlic Aura. In-game card: “Wide aura slows the swarm, kills heal you”.

Contents
  1. 1 Recipe
  2. 2 What changes
  3. 3 Playing it

Recipe

Garlic Aura at level 5 + Regeneration at any level. Once both are in your build, the evolution card appears on the next level-up with an overwhelming weight (it is practically always one of the three cards), and the next chest evolves the weapon automatically if you open one first. Evolving does not use a slot and keeps the passive.

What changes

A wider aura that slows every non-boss enemy inside it to 60% speed, and every kill it makes heals 1 HP. Kill-heals share a bucket of 2 HP per second with Soul Siphon.

Nominal output without bonuses grows from 33 to 49 damage per second (×1.5; see Tier list for what this number measures for each weapon).

Playing it

  • The aura only works when enemies touch you, so it is a defensive weapon: it thins out what gets close. Add ranged damage early, or the swarm and the spitters outside the aura will wear you down.
  • Small ticks are the first thing late armor eats; damage bonuses matter a lot for the aura.
