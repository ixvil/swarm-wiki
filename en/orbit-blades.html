Orbit Blades is one of the eight weapons in Swarm Survivor. In-game card: “2 blades circle around you”. It levels up to 5 and evolves into Blade Storm with Big Swings.

How it works

Blades circle the hero on a fixed orbit and damage every enemy they touch. A blade can hit the same enemy again only after 0.4 s (a fixed re-hit timer: Quick Hands does not shorten it). Every hit knocks the enemy back, so the orbit works as a moving fence against small enemies.

Area bonuses widen the orbit and thicken the blades, but the orbit's outer edge is capped at 107 units (about half the screen width), and blade size grows at most ×1.3.

Evolution

Reach level 5 and own Big Swings at any level: the next level-up offers the Blade Storm card, or the next chest evolves it automatically. Six heavy blades on a wider orbit that spins faster. Big damage per touch keeps it useful after 5:00, when enemies grow flat armor.

Tactics

  • Blades protect you from the small stuff that reaches you: bats, slimes, skeletons, ghosts. They do not move Elites (elites ignore knockback) and brutes resist half of it, so keep kiting instead of standing inside the orbit.
  • Pair with a ranged weapon: the orbit never reaches spitters, which keep 150–230 units away.

How to get it

Survive 8:00 with Homing Darts; or buy for 2,400 coins once Moonlit Meadow is reached.

See Collection and weapon unlocks for how locked weapons work.
