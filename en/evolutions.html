Evolution recipes

From the Swarm Survivor: Horde Hunter Wiki · data from game version 1.7.2

Every weapon has one evolution. The rule is the same for all eight: get the weapon to level 5 and own its partner passive at any level. From that moment the evolution card is in the level-up pool with a weight of 10,000 (an ordinary upgrade weighs 10), so in practice it shows up on the very next level-up, and a chest opened first evolves the weapon automatically.

Conditions and details

  • The passive does not have to be maxed — level 1 is enough.
  • Passives can be taken before or after the weapon reaches level 5; order does not matter.
  • Evolving does not take a slot and keeps the passive's own bonus.
  • If two weapons are ready at once, both evolution cards can appear together; a chest evolves the first eligible weapon in your loadout order.
  • Relic screens (every 10th level) never show evolution cards — the evolution waits for the next normal level-up.
  • Three passives are locked at first: Clover (needs Lightning), Magnet (needs Mines) and Critical Eye (needs Laser). See Collection and weapon unlocks.
  • Spinach and Iron Heart are not partners of any weapon.
  • In the Purist challenge there are no passives, so nothing can evolve.
