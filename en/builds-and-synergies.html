Builds and synergies

From the Swarm Survivor: Horde Hunter Wiki · data from game version 1.7.2

A build is six weapon slots and six passive slots filled from random cards, so plan in priorities rather than fixed lists. The synergies below follow directly from how the stats stack.

Synergies that follow from the numbers

  • Cooldown stacking: Quick Hands 5 (×0.6) × Vega the Stormcaller (×0.92) × Overclock (×0.82) = ×0.45 — more than double the fire rate of every cooldown weapon, and a faster aura tick.
  • Projectile count: Twin Souls adds a projectile to six weapons at once; the more of Blades, Darts, Boomerang, Lightning, Mines and Laser you carry, the bigger the gain.
  • Damage multiplies: Spinach 5 (×1.6) × Fury (×1.3) × Might 5 (×1.4) = ×2.91, and Critical Eye 5 adds about ×1.45 on average on top. Damage is applied before the enemies' flat armor, so it is worth even more after 5:00.
  • Defense up to the cap: Armor + Plating + Bulwark or Bram the Monk all multiply, but stop at 60%; past that, put points into HP (Iron Heart, Vitality) and Regeneration instead.
  • Speed and boomerangs: Swift Boots evolve Boomerang and make kiting easier, and Nyx the Rogue adds her own +8%.
  • Luck and chests: Clover evolves Lightning and raises the chance that a chest gives three upgrades.
  • Range + guard: pair one weapon that kills far away with one that protects you up close; spitters stay 150–230 units away, outside any aura or orbit.

Example builds

Storm and stone (boss killer)

Hero: Vega the Stormcaller · Weapons: Lightning, Mines, Boomerang · Passives: Clover, Magnet, Spinach, Quick Hands

Evolutions: Storm Lord, Gravity Mines. Two S-tier evolutions with big hits that ignore late armor. Twin Souls and Fury are the dream relics.

Fortress

Hero: Bram the Monk · Weapons: Garlic Aura, Shockwave, Boomerang · Passives: Regeneration, Armor, Iron Heart, Spinach

Evolutions: Soul Aura, Earthquake. Soul Aura slows and heals, Earthquake pushes and stuns, the boomerang deals with spitters. Defense to the cap, then HP.

Arcane gunner

Hero: Ash the Knight · Weapons: Homing Darts, Orbit Blades, Lightning · Passives: Quick Hands, Big Swings, Spinach, Clover

Evolutions: Arcane Barrage, Blade Storm, Storm Lord. Arcane Barrage plus Blade Storm covers far and near; Spinach keeps the light darts relevant after 7:00.

Whirlwind

Hero: Nyx the Rogue · Weapons: Boomerang, Laser, Mines · Passives: Swift Boots, Critical Eye, Magnet, Spinach

Evolutions: Cyclone, Prism Ray, Gravity Mines. Cyclone and Prism Ray cover every direction; three dash charges with Phantom Step make Nyx very hard to pin down.

These builds need some weapons unlocked first (see Collection and weapon unlocks). They are examples assembled from the numbers on this wiki, not measured win rates.

Priorities by phase

Early game (0:00–3:00)
Get to 2–3 weapons (one ranged, one close), level your main weapon, grab its partner passive. Enemies are softened in these minutes, so greed for levels is fine.
Mid game (3:00–6:00)
Aim for the first evolution around the Slime King (its chest evolves a ready weapon). Add one defensive passive. Brutes and elites arrive at 4:00; flat armor at 5:00.
Late game (6:00–10:00)
Second (and third) evolution, damage multipliers against armor, defense to the cap, mobility. See Surviving 7–10 minutes and Boss preparation.
