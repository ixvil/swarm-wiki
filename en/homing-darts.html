Homing Darts is one of the eight weapons in Swarm Survivor. In-game card: “Fires a dart at the nearest enemy”. It levels up to 5 and evolves into Arcane Barrage with Quick Hands.

How it works

Every 0.64 s at level 5 (from 0.85 s at level 1) the hero throws a volley of darts at the nearest enemies within 440 units: each dart picks the next-nearest target, so a volley spreads over several enemies. Darts steer toward their target, fly at 380 units/s, live 1.6 s and pass through as many enemies as their pierce value.

Evolution

Reach level 5 and own Quick Hands at any level: the next level-up offers the Arcane Barrage card, or the next chest evolves it automatically. A near-constant stream of six piercing darts. The best pure auto-aim in the game; each dart is light, so it loses the most to late armor.

Tactics

  • The safest opener: darts aim themselves, so you can focus entirely on movement. They kill the nearest threats first, which is exactly what keeps you alive in the first minutes.
  • Each dart hits lightly, so from 5:00 the flat armor of new enemies eats a large share of it. Back darts up with damage (Spinach, Fury) or with a heavy-hitting second weapon.

How to get it

Available from the start. It is also the starting weapon of Ash the Knight.
