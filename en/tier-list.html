Tier list

From the Swarm Survivor: Horde Hunter Wiki · data from game version 1.7.2

This tier list is built from the game's own numbers: damage per hit, output per second, reach, and how much survives the flat armor regulars get after 5:00. It is a reasoned ranking for endless runs, not a measured win rate — all eight evolutions can carry a run.

Contents
  1. 1 How to read the numbers
  2. 2 Evolutions
  3. 3 Passives
  4. 4 Relics

How to read the numbers

  • Nominal output: damage per second without any bonus. For darts, boomerangs, mines and lightning it is all projectiles together (not all will always hit); for blades, aura and shockwave it is per enemy in reach; for the laser, per enemy in one beam.
  • At 9:00: what one hit deals to a regular that spawned at 9:00 (flat armor 18), without and with Spinach 5 + Fury (×2.08).

Passives

TierPassivesReasoning
SSpinach, Quick HandsMultiply everything you deal; Spinach also beats late armor, Quick Hands speeds up every weapon.
AArmor, Regeneration, Iron Heart, Big SwingsSurvival (60% reduction cap, 6 HP/s at level 5, ×1.9 HP) and area (diminishing, capped).
BCritical Eye, Swift Boots, CloverGood, but weaker than Spinach per level (crit ≈ +8% damage per level) or situational.
CMagnetComfort only — unless you own Mines, where it is the evolution key.

Exception that beats any tier: the partner passive of a weapon you already have at a high level — the evolution is worth more than the stat.

Relics

TierRelicsReasoning
SFury, Twin Souls+30% damage for everything; +1 projectile for six weapons.
AOverclock, Bulwark, Guardian Heart, Phantom StepStrong all-rounders: faster fire, a big HP buffer with a full heal, a stronger automatic nova, extra dash.
BColossus, Soul SiphonArea hits diminishing returns and caps; kill-healing is capped at 2 HP/s.

Situational picks override the list: take Bulwark when the relic screen comes at low HP, Phantom Step if you rely on dashes, Twin Souls only if you carry several count-based weapons.
