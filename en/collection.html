Collection and weapon unlocks

From the Swarm Survivor: Horde Hunter Wiki · data from game version 1.7.2

A new player starts with three weapons — Homing Darts, Garlic Aura, Boomerang — and earns the other five. Locked weapons are never offered on level-up cards. The Collection screen on the menu shows every weapon, its progress and its evolution (shown as “???” until you have evolved it once).

Rules

  • Goals are checked once a minute during a run and at its end, so an unlock can happen mid-run (a “NEW WEAPON!” banner) and the weapon can be offered right away.
  • “Survive 8:00 with Homing Darts”: darts must be in your loadout; your best time with darts is tracked.
  • “Defeat the Swarm Queen in the Meadow” is checked with the world progress saved before the victory screen, so Lightning actually unlocks at the first check after it: one minute into your next endless run (Vega and the Desert open immediately).
  • “Defeat 15,000 Skeletons” counts skeletons over all your endless runs.
  • Pedestals appear only in their world, at their time, while the weapon is locked; the Ruins one at 12:00 needs the Lich to still be alive.
  • The shop price opens once you have reached the world listed (cleared the world before it).
  • A newly unlocked weapon is guaranteed once on the first card screen of the next run, if you have a free weapon slot.
  • Three passives open together with their weapon: Clover with Lightning, Magnet with Mines, Critical Eye with Laser.
  • Challenge rounds ignore locks: any weapon can be offered there.
