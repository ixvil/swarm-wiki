Is the game free? Do I have to watch ads?

Free. Rewarded videos are always optional. An interstitial ad shows between runs only after a run of 4:00 or longer, when you tap PLAY AGAIN or Home — never during a run and never after challenge rounds. See Ads.

How do I remove ads?

Buy Remove ads or the starter pack. Both remove interstitial ads for good; optional reward videos stay available if you want them.

Can I play without paying?

Yes. Every hero, weapon, world and upgrade can be earned by playing. Purchases only speed things up or remove interstitials.

What does the starter pack contain?

1,500 coins plus the removal of interstitial ads. It is offered once (after your first boss kill or a 5-minute run) and then stays in the shop.

I reinstalled the game / got a new phone. Is my progress gone?

Progress is stored on the device, with no cloud save. Android's own backup may restore it. Purchases (Remove ads, the starter pack's ad removal) come back with Restore purchases. See Saving and progress.

I beat the Swarm Queen but Lightning is still locked.

It unlocks at the next check: one minute into your next endless run you will see “NEW WEAPON!”. Vega and the Desert unlock immediately. See Collection and weapon unlocks.

Why can't I find Clover, Magnet or Critical Eye?

They open together with Lightning, Mines and Laser respectively.

What happens at 10:00?

The world's final boss arrives. Kill it to clear the world; there is no time limit after that — the run goes on until the boss or you fall.

Why do I take so much damage when I stand still?

Standing in one spot for more than 3 s multiplies contact damage up to ×8 at 8 s and stops your base regeneration. See Keep moving.

How does the revive work?

Once per endless run, on the defeat screen: pay 120 coins or watch an optional video. You come back with 50% HP, 2.5 s of invulnerability, and a blast that stuns everything within 320 units and deals 60% of their max HP (10% to bosses).

Is there a time limit or energy system?

No energy, no waiting. Play as many runs as you like.

Does the game send notifications?

No.

Do I need an internet connection?

The game itself plays offline. Ads (and their rewards) and purchases need a connection.

Where are the numbers on this wiki from?

They are generated from the game code (game version 1.7.2).
