Surviving 7–10 minutes

From the Swarm Survivor: Horde Hunter Wiki · data from game version 1.7.2

Minutes 7 to 10 are the hardest part of every world. It is not one mechanic but several stacking at once:

  • Density reaches ×1.5 at 5:00 and the alive cap of 340 regulars is hit from about 6:00.
  • The on-screen minimum keeps 39 regulars in view at 7:00 and 46 at 9:00: killing fast never empties the screen.
  • New regulars carry flat armor (9 at 7:00, 18 at 9:00), which weak hits barely get through.
  • If you kill fast, the surge makes new regulars up to 5× tougher and swaps bats and slimes in edge bursts for chargers and spitters.
  • Crowd hits grow: each contact hit also carries up to 55% of the other touching enemies' damage.
  • Scripted pressure: elite brute at 7:00, a 12-brute pack at 7:30, an elite skeleton and a thief at 8:20, a 36-ghost ring plus 40 bats at 9:00.
  • Rocks fall more often and in bigger groups from 6:00.

What to do

  1. Bring big hits. Mines, Lightning, Boomerang and their evolutions keep their damage against armor; light weapons need Spinach, Fury and Might.
  2. Defense to 60% before 7:00 if you can: armor counts fully against contact hits, which is what the crowd does.
  3. Keep a lane. Do not let the swarm close all sides; move toward the emptiest side early (the game keeps one open).
  4. Save a dash for the brute pack and the 9:00 ring.
  5. Pick up supply hearts even if it means a detour — they heal 30%.
  6. Never camp. Standing still turns crowd hits into ×8 hits.
If your build is weak, the game quietly helps between 3:00 and 10:00 (softer regulars), but only while you keep moving. Moving is never wrong.
